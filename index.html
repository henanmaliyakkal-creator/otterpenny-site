Why another money app

You already know. Doing is the hard part.

Most money apps are ledgers. They work only if you remember to log, and they show you the damage at month end, when nothing can change. Otterpenny works at the moment you spend: the ₹300 order, the 2 a.m. cart, the guilty ₹500 card payment.

How it works

Four things, zero homework

  1. 01

    It logs itself

    Otterpenny reads the SMS and app alerts your bank already sends, works out the amount and the shop, and files the spend. Cash takes two taps. A wrong category takes one.

  2. 02

    One number a day

    Rent, bills, SIPs and the card payment come off the top first. What's left, split across the days, is today's safe amount. Freelance income that lands in lumps is paid out to you as a steady monthly salary.

  3. 03

    A nudge, not a lecture

    On your fourth delivery order of the week, the alert shows what this week has cost and what the same money would do for the card. You still decide.

  4. 04

    Your garden grows

    Habits grow plants: looking at your day, a no-delivery day, noting how you felt. A bad day never kills one. It just waits for tomorrow.

Questions

Straight answers

What is Otterpenny?

Otterpenny is a free Android money app built for ADHD brains. It logs your spending by itself from bank alerts, gives you one safe number to spend each day, and turns credit card debt into one planned payment a month.

How does Otterpenny track expenses automatically?

It reads the SMS and app alerts your bank already sends after a UPI or card payment, works out the amount and the shop, and files the spend under a category. Cash takes two taps to add, and fixing a wrong category takes one.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. Otterpenny has no sign-up, no servers, no cloud sync, no analytics and no ads. Alerts are read on your phone and stored in a SQLCipher-encrypted database on the device. Backups are an encrypted file you save wherever you choose.

Which banks does Otterpenny support?

HDFC Bank alerts are supported today, with more bank formats coming. You can also import a bank statement.

Is Otterpenny available on iPhone?

No. iOS does not let apps read bank SMS, so automatic logging is not possible on iPhone. Otterpenny needs Android 10 or newer.

How much does Otterpenny cost?

Otterpenny is free, with no ads and no subscription.
